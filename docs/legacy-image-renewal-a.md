# Legacy train image renewal A

Seven new quiz illustrations were generated from scratch with the built-in image generator. The legacy files were inspected to identify their pictured vehicle variants; the Azusa illustration was inspected for style. Accepted images were copied only from the exact local path returned by their own generation call. Every copied PNG was opened for visual review. Each WebP was exported from its matching PNG at quality 88, method 6.

All final files are 1536 × 1024 pixels. The PNG and WebP dimensions were checked after export.
