# Environment lighting

The three bundled 1K Radiance HDR files are CC0 assets from Poly Haven. They are
used as reflection/illumination references, not as geographical backdrops.

- `small_hangar_01.hdr`: [Small Hangar 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/small_hangar_01), Sergej Majboroda.
- `shanghai_bund.hdr`: [Shanghai Bund](https://polyhaven.com/a/shanghai_bund), Greg Zaal.
- `railway_bridge_02.hdr`: [Railway Bridge 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/railway_bridge_02).

[Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license): CC0, including redistribution.
[CC0 1.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode).

The application loads local copies; it makes no runtime requests to Poly Haven.
